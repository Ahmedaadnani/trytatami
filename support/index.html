Tatami Unlimited

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Profile → Tatami Unlimited and tap Restore. Purchases are also tied to your Sign in with Apple account, so they usually come back as soon as you sign in.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Profile → Tatami Unlimited → Manage subscription, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tatami. You'll keep Unlimited until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What's included in the free version?

3 quizzes a day (5 or 10 questions), 10 Discover cards a day and up to 5 saved moves, plus The Path, the Library, your streak and Find a Dojo. Unlimited removes the limits and adds your stats and the Move of the Day widget.

Using Tatami

My progress isn't showing on my other device

Tatami syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID on both devices with iCloud turned on, and that you've signed in to Tatami on each. Changes can take a minute to appear.

How do I change my belt or the app's colour?

Open Profile → Customisation → Your Belt. Tatami takes on your belt's colour, and The Path starts from it.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

Profile → the gear icon → Notifications. You can pick a time, and reminders skip days you've already opened Tatami. If they're blocked, allow notifications for Tatami in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

How do I add the Move of the Day widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Tatami and choose a size. The widget is part of Tatami Unlimited.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Profile → the gear icon → Delete Account. This erases your progress, favourites, preferences and profile from your device and iCloud. If you have a subscription, cancel it separately in iPhone Settings so you're not charged again.

What information does Tatami keep?

Very little, and none of it on our own servers. See the Privacy Policy.
