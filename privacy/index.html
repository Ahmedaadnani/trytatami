Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: Tatami has no servers of its own. Your account, progress and preferences stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

This policy explains how the Tatami app ("Tatami", "we", "us") handles your information. Tatami is made by Ahmed Al-Adnani in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller. If you have any questions, email support@trytatami.com.

Information Tatami uses

Your account (Sign in with Apple)

Tatami uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app a unique identifier for your account and, the first time you sign in and only if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be a private relay address). You can also add a profile photo and change your name in the app.

This is stored on your device and in your private iCloud storage, so it's there when you sign in on your other devices. It is not sent to us or kept on any server we run.

Your learning

Your lesson progress, saved techniques, daily log-ins and streak, quiz results, chosen belt, settings and your answer to "How did you hear about Tatami?" are stored on your device and synced through your iCloud. Daily limits for the free tier are counted on your device only.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are paid for through Apple. We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and to know whether you have Tatami Unlimited. RevenueCat receives your Sign in with Apple identifier (or a random identifier if you're signed out), your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical information such as your device model, iOS and app version, country and IP address. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf to provide the service.

Reminders and the widget

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. The Move of the Day widget only learns, on your device, whether you have Tatami Unlimited.

What Tatami doesn't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of your information.
  • No access to your location, contacts or camera. Photos are only read if you choose one for your profile.

Why we use it

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), we use your information to provide the app and the purchases you choose to make (performance of a contract), and to keep the app working properly and securely (our legitimate interests).

How long it's kept

Information on your device and in your iCloud stays until you delete it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This erases your progress, favourites, preferences and profile from your device and your iCloud.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Tatami.
  • Turn off reminders in the app's Settings, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
  • You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, and to data portability. To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, email us and we'll help.

If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Tatami is suitable for judoka of all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what's described above. If you're a parent or guardian with a question, please contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
